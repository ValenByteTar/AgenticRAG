--- Página 3 ---
Imagine you could hire thousands of
geniuses who have a PDF in everything,
imagine they never sleep, complete
complex tasks in seconds, cost pennies
per hour, they can speak every language,
they get smarter with every project they
complete and every month their
intelligence improves by 15%± per month
automatically - welcome to the
astonishing world of AI agents.
An AI agent is a bit of computer software
that acts like a smart employee: you tell it
what you want, and it figures out the
steps, and it gets on with the job itself.

--- Página 4 ---
Klarna built an AI-agent call-centre. Their AI-
Agents have so far handled 2.3 million calls in 1
month. Resolving 70%± of calls in under two
minutes – the work of about 700 human staff and
worth an extra $40 million in profit this year. 
T H E  M O S T  I M P O R T A N T  S K I L L  T O  L E A R N
I’ve used AI-agents this week to do the following:
Order online: I told my agent to buy
supplements, electronics and clothes. Within an
hour the doorbell rang - everything had arrived.
All I did was ask; the agent found the items,
paid, tipped the driver and filled in my address.
Build software: I asked my agent for tools to
boost my productivity. It designed and
launched two apps tailored to my workflow that
I now use every day.

--- Página 5 ---
In an age of AI agents, creative judgement
becomes even more important - a mix of
good taste, vivid imagination, narrative
ability, bias for action, disciplined work ethic
and comfort with constant change.
When machines can spit out endless options
and complete endless tasks, the winners will
be those who recognise what a good idea is,
shape it into a clear instruction, take
decisive initiative and keep learning as the
technology rapidly evolves. 
In a world of infinite answers, 
you need to know the right question.

--- Página 6 ---
I’m reading more on human psychology.
Technology keeps shifting, but our motivations,
needs and human wiring will remain the same.
When you understand what moves people, you
can create things that serve them.
I’m casting a wider net in what I read and watch
– science, art, sport, history, tech. Creativity is
the skill of joining uncommon dots. The more
varied and abundant the dots, the richer the
ideas you can form.
I’m experimenting. Reading is useful. But you
learn more by messing around with something,
experimenting and getting hands on. When I
hear about a new AI breakthrough, I lean in - I
try and use it straight away - even when it’s
confusing or complicated.

--- Página 7 ---
Keep asking why and how.
Make friends with the machines - don’t avoid them -
but remember they cannot dream like you can,
relate to others like you can or feel like you can.
Continue to read, write, build things, draw and talk
to people outside of your street, echo-chamber and
community.
Learn to understand people and then learn how to
tell stories, because stories are how humans guide
machines, not the other way around. 
Be a good human being, because regardless of how
productive you become, nothing will make you
richer than the strength and depth of the
relationships you build.
