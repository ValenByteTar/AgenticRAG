--- Página 2 ---
Tell me about a situation where you had to work effectively under tight
deadlines or pressure.
Describe a project or task where you had to solve complex problems and
make tough decisions.
How do you manage your time and priorities at work?
Tell me about a situation where you had to collaborate with a difficult
colleague or resolve a conflict within a team.
What were your contributions and achievements in previous projects or roles?
How do you set and achieve goals? Can you provide a specific example?
Describe a situation where you had to adapt to changes in working conditions
or new project requirements.
How do you develop your professional skills and stay updated on the latest
trends in your field?
Describe a situation where you had to lead a project or a team. What was the
outcome?
How do you build and maintain relationships with clients, colleagues, and
other stakeholders?
How do you react to criticism, and how do you use it for your personal and
professional growth?
How do you ensure effective communication within a team and with other
departments?
Tell me about a situation where you had to train or mentor a new employee.
What were the results?
How do you approach task delegation within a team? Can you provide an
example of successful delegation?
Describe a situation where you had to solve a problem for which there was no
clear solution or standard approach.

How do you support and motivate your team to achieve common goals?
Tell me about a moment when you had to overcome your own
limitations or address your weaknesses.
How do you handle conflicts within a team, and what methods do you
use to find compromises?
How do you respond to failures or unsuccessful project outcomes?
What was your experience in learning from such situations?
Describe a situation where you had to make a significant decision with
limited information. What was the outcome?
How do you develop and maintain corporate culture within your team or
workplace?
Tell me about a situation where you had to effectively motivate yourself
or your team during a challenging period.
Think about a difficult boss, professor, or coworker. How did you
successfully interact with this person?
What is your greatest work-related accomplishment to date?
