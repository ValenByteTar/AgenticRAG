Whaling is a highly targeted phishing
attack - aimed at senior executives -
masquerading as a legitimate email.  This
is also known as “executive phishing.”
TYPES
TYPES
WHALING
WHALING
PREVENTION:
If you receive a suspicious email from a
coworker, reach out to them directly to
confirm its legitimacy.

--- Página 5 ---
“Spear phishing”is a type of
phishing campaign that targets a
specific person or group and often will
include information known to be of
interest to the target, such as current
events or financial documents.
SPEAR PHISHING
SPEAR PHISHING
PREVENTION:
Users should always double-check the
sender's email address before giving a
response or responding to the mails.

--- Página 6 ---
Pharming is online fraud that involves
the use of malicious code to direct
victims to spoofed websites in an
attempt to steal their credentials and
data. 
PHARMING
PHARMING
PREVENTION:
Avoid visiting unsecure
“HTTP” websites.

--- Página 7 ---
DECEPTIVE PHISHING
DECEPTIVE PHISHING
This type of phishing uses deceptive
technology such as email spoofing to send
messages that appear to be from an
address other than their own. Like other
types of phishing emails, these messages
may contain malicious links or attachments
PREVENTION:
 Always use a good antivirus
and also think twice before
clicking links and attachments.

--- Página 8 ---
HTTPS phishing is when a cybercriminal
tricks you into giving up your personal
information using a malicious website.
To get you onto these sites, the phisher
will hide the malicious link within an
email, often masquerading as a link to a
legitimate site.
HTTPS PHISHING
HTTPS PHISHING
PREVENTION:
Always look closely at the URL of
a site before logging in.

--- Página 9 ---
Social engineering is the tactic of
manipulating, influencing, or deceiving
a victim in order to gain control over a
computer system, or to steal personal
and financial information.
SOCIAL ENGINEERING
SOCIAL ENGINEERING
PREVENTION:
Avoid panicking and take your time to
assess the legitimacy of the situation.

--- Página 10 ---
VISHING
VISHING
Vishing is short for "voice phishing," which
involves defrauding people over the phone,
enticing them to divulge sensitive
information over the phone.
PREVENTION:
 Always use a good antivirus
and also think twice before
clicking links and attachments.

--- Página 11 ---
Pop-up phishing is a type of attack that
leverages adware and pop-up ads to
trick users into downloading malware
onto their devices
POP-UP PHISHING
POP-UP PHISHING
PREVENTION:
Enable a pop-up blocker and avoid
clicking on any popup windows you
encounter online.

--- Página 12 ---
Search engine phishing attacks attract
users using fake product pages. The catch
is that instead of being able to purchase
the product, they’re handing over their
payment information to a scammer.
SEARCH ENGINE
PHISHING
SEARCH ENGINE
PHISHING
PREVENTION:
Avoid giving your payment information
to websites other than trusted and
reputable online vendors.

--- Página 13 ---
IMAGE PHISHING
IMAGE PHISHING
Image phishing is an attack in which
hackers disguise malicious code or
different types of malwares using image
files. Once you click on the image, your
computer will begin downloading the
malicious code stored within the image. 
PREVENTION:
Never click on or download an
image from a suspicious email
message.

--- Página 14 ---
Smishing is the word that describes
phishing over short message services
(SMS). Phishing text messages usually
use social engineering tactics and
contain malicious links. 
SMISHING
SMISHING
PREVENTION:
Avoid opening links from unknown
phone numbers.

--- Página 15 ---
Domain spoofing is a type of phishing
attack in which the attacker impersonates
a known person or company using a fake
email domain.
DOMAIN SPOOFING
DOMAIN SPOOFING
PREVENTION:
Copy the sender’s email address and
compare it to the official email address
listed on the company’s site.

--- Página 16 ---
MAN IN THE MIDDLE
MAN IN THE MIDDLE
A man-in-the-middle (MITM) attack is an
attack in which a hacker steals your
information by getting in between you and
a trusted party.
PREVENTION:
Always use a VPN when
connecting to public Wi-Fi
networks.
