his repository is architecture-driven.

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) are the authoritative source of truth for all architectural decisions.

Before implementing any change you must:

Read every relevant ADR.
Understand the architectural intent.
Verify your implementation complies with all accepted ADRs.
Never implement an alternative architecture because it appears simpler.
Never bypass architectural layers.
Never merge responsibilities that are intentionally separated.

If an ADR appears incorrect or outdated:

DO NOT change the implementation.

Instead:

explain the issue,
describe the trade-offs,
propose a new ADR,
wait for approval.

Architecture consistency always has higher priority than implementation speed.
